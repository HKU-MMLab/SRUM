Fine-Grained Self-Rewarding for Unified Multimodal Models

Close the understanding–generation gap by letting a UMM’s own understanding module reward its generation — without human labels.

Abstract

Unified Multimodal Models integrate understanding and generation, yet strong visual understanding often fails to transfer to generation. SRUM builds an offline self-rewarding loop: the frozen understanding module evaluates generated candidates with a global–local dual reward, then guides generation updates through reward-weighted flow matching — no additional human-labeled data.

How it works

01

Understand

Freeze the UMM understanding side as an offline evaluator. No evaluator gradients.

02

Dual reward

Global score α gates a local region map R for layout and object-level fidelity.

03

Improve

Update generation / flow parameters with \(L_r + \lambda_c L_{\mathrm{ref}}\) (\(\lambda_c=0.5\)).
