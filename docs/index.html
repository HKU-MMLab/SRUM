SRUM: Fine-Grained Self-Rewarding for Unified Multimodal Models

Let a unified model’s understanding module reward its own generation — closing the understanding–generation gap without extra human labels.

Weiyang Jin*, Yuwei Niu*, Jiaqi Liao, Chengqi Duan, Aoxue Li, Shenghua Gao, Xihui Liu

The University of Hong Kong

Paper Model Code PDF

Abstract

Remarkable progress has been made in Unified Multimodal Models (UMMs), which integrate vision-language generation and understanding within a single framework. Yet a model’s strong visual understanding often fails to transfer to visual generation. SRUM creates a feedback loop where the model’s own understanding module acts as an internal evaluator, providing corrective signals to improve generation without additional human-labeled data. A global–local dual reward offers multi-scale guidance: a global reward for overall semantics and layout, and a local reward for object-level fidelity. SRUM boosts T2I-CompBench from 82.18 to 88.37 and T2I-ReasonBench from 43.82 to 46.75.

Method

01 · Understand

Freeze the UMM understanding side as an offline evaluator (no evaluator gradients).

02 · Dual reward

Global score α gates a local region map R for multi-scale self-rewarding.

03 · Improve generation

Update generation / flow parameters with reward-weighted flow matching + reference constraint.

Offline recipe: ~6K candidates · AdamW · lr 2.5e-5 · warmup 500 · λc = 0.5
